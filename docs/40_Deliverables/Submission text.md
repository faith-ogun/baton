# Submission text

**Audience:** hackathon judges, reading fast, not domain experts
**Where it goes:** the submission portal
**Deadline:** 15:30 CEST, 12 September 2026

## The one-liner

A coworker that makes sure nothing gets dropped. Baton maps your team's real workflow, spots the
stalled ask, the overloaded person, the unbooked deadline and the single point of failure, then
rescues each one with a governed, one-click hand-off across your apps.

## Lead with the named user and the number

Rachel Foster leads regulatory affairs at Aldermere Bio. Eight people, one filing date. On Monday
morning Baton shows her five things about to drop, worth **£43.2k in delayed work**, and the top one
is an ask she never knew existed because she was not on the thread. Five minutes and three
approvals later, team health has moved from 52 to 71.

## The four things to say, in order

1. **What gets dropped.** Four failures, four detectors, four rules. Three are purely
   deterministic.
2. **Why it cannot be a chatbox.** A chatbot answers the question you thought to ask; every risk
   here is one you did not. Three of the four are invisible from inside any single app.
3. **How it is governed.** Nothing auto-sends, ever. The model writes sentences; a registry you can
   read decides everything else, and moving a threshold re-scores the queue in front of you.
4. **How deep the integration goes.** Provisioned as a workspace member, subscribes to the
   webhooks, reads all six surfaces, acts back into them under its own identity, and writes its
   audit log into a Sheet **inside the workspace**, which is also the persistence layer.

## What it must not say

Everything ruled out by [[Demo facts]]. Specifically:

- not "catches 70%". Say **built to catch**
- not "connected to a live workspace" over the seeded state. The header says `seeded workspace`
- not the Asana or PMI figures unless they have been verified
- never "deterministic" on camera. Say **cannot go differently on a second run**
