# Callout conventions

Obsidian callouts are how a note flags something a skim-reader must not miss. Four types only;
more than four and they stop meaning anything.

## `> [!warning]`

Something that will cost you if you ignore it. A claim that is not verified, a number that must not
be stated as fact, a path that breaks the demo.

> [!warning] Not verified
> The Asana and PMI figures in the cost section are quoted from memory. Check or cut before
> recording.

## `> [!info]`

Context a reader needs but which is not a risk. Background, a definition, a pointer.

> [!info] What "open loop" means here
> A message that is a request directed at a specific person, with no reply from that person after
> the ask, older than the threshold in the registry.

## `> [!success]`

A thing that is genuinely done and verified, with the evidence. Never aspirational.

> [!success] The approve loop runs end to end
> Verified in the browser: health 52 to 59, four nodes cooled, three open edges closed, audit row
> written.

## `> [!question]`

An open question with a named owner and, ideally, a date. A question with no owner is a wish.

> [!question] Does Cloud Run get done?
> Stretch only. Faith decides at 15:10 whether there is time.
