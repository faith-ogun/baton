# Hackathon overview

**Event:** "Agents, Everywhere: Bots, Channels & More", AI Tinkerers x OpenAI, global.
**Date:** Saturday 12 September 2026. One build window, **11:15 to 15:30 CEST**.
**Entrant:** Faith Ogundimu, solo.
**Target:** Best Use of Ambiguous AI (prize: NVIDIA DGX Spark), while also competing for 1st
overall (OpenAI credits).

## The net-new rule

Nothing may be built before 11:15. Planning documents are fine; feature code is not. The first
feature commit is timestamped after the bell, and the asset preparation that ran from 11:04 was
image processing on the logo board, not product code.

## The four criteria, scored 1 to 5

| Criterion | How Baton answers it |
|---|---|
| **Core requirements and functionality** | a provisioned coworker that reads the workspace and takes real actions in it. The loop runs end to end on camera |
| **Innovation and theme alignment** | an agent that perceives the whole org graph and intervenes to prevent dropped work. Say the "cannot be a chatbox" line explicitly |
| **Technical execution and integration** | real webhooks, a deterministic graph and risk engine, an executor that writes back, an audit trail, retries and idempotency |
| **Usefulness and agentic experience** | human-in-the-loop on every action, native identity, full control, and a named user with a number |

## The moat

Deep Ambiguous use. Provisioned as a coworker, subscribed to webhooks, reading across all apps,
acting across all apps, and writing its audit log back into the workspace as a Sheet. That is the
deepest available integration, and it is what wins the category.
